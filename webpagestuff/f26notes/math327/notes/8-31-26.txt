Sample Space
S= collection of all possible outcomes. Event E = subset of sample space.
An event occurs, means that one of the outcomes happen. 
0/ (zero with line through) an event without any outcome, This is called 
a Null event

Experiment: any process is an event where the outcome cannot be predicted

A continuous sample space could be
S={X|X>=0}, this example is a sample of any value between 0 and infinity
Event could be
A={X|X>=6}, this event shows every outcome greater or equal to 6
B={X|3<=x<=10}, an event that is between 3 and 10 inclusively

A sample with multiple types of outcomes must be split into specific outcomes
For example a bag with multicolored, multinumbered balls, the sample space
depends on whether we are interested in color or value
ie Sc={Red, Green, Blue}, Sn={1,2,...,42}
A sample spcae can also be both, ie S={R1,R2,...,G1,G2,...B1,B2,...}

Compliment of an event A, denoted by A^c, is all of the outcomes not in A
ex: S={1,2,3,4,5,6}, A={1,3,5}, A^c={2,4,6}, 
think of as a logical not operator

Intersection of A and B, denoted by AnB (Upside down U), Includes outcomes in
A and B, Think of as a logical AND
ex: S={1,2,3,4,5,6}, A={1,2,3,4}, B={2,4,6}, AnB={2,4}
If there is no intersection, the result is Null Event, A and B are mutually 
exclusive

Union of A and B, denoted by AUB, Includes outcomes in
A ior B, Think of as a logical iOR
ex: S={1,2,3,4,5,6}, A={1,2,3,4}, B={2,4,6}, AUB={1,2,3,4,6}

DeMorgans Law: 
(AUB)^c= A^c n B^c
(AnB)^c= A^c U B^c
