Reproductive Patterns and survivorship:
r-Strategists: Make lots of offspring, but with fewer resources, 
Quantity not Quality
K-startegists: Produce few young but invest lots of resources:
Quality over Quantity
Humans are 100% K strategists

          r               |          K
========================================================
 small organisms          | Larger organisms
 little energy to produce | Lots of energy per offspring
 many offspring           | Few offspring produced
 early maturity           | Late Maturity
 short life expectancy    | Long life expectancy

Genetic Diversity
Variety of genes in a population
Cause of sexual reproduction, easier for a species to survive with varied 
genetic offspring
More adaptable to environmental change
More stable ecosystems that can react to change overall

Florida Panther:
Low genetic diversity
In 1970s only 30 left
Lots of inbreeding and birth defects
Took a similar species, texas pumas, and introduced them into the area
They had poor genetics, but the genetic diversity alone helped the population
grow 

Purebred dogs
Most purebred dogs have high rates of genetic defects because the genetics
dont vary much, mutts are generally healthier

Smaller population easier to be wiped out drom a disease
Genetic drift, gradual changes to gene frequency
Bottleneck effect: event occurs that reduces population of one specific 
allele type, surviving reproduction leads to less of the bottlenecked gene, 
lower population of a certian trait

Island Biogeography:
Small islands with small populations, more vulnerable to extinction, less 
genetic variety because there are less different members to pass on different
genes
Larger islands are more stable and biodiverse

Metapopulations:
Gene flow 
Originally developed to describe insect pest population, 
but extends across species

Human infrastructure can inhibit population mixing, fragmented, isolated 
populations
