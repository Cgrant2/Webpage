Scientific Method overview/review

A lot of our knowledge of the planet as humans came from direct experience, trial and error

Scientific method:
-----------------
1: Identify question (Consult prior knowledge)
2: Form a testable hypothesis
3: Collect data to test hypothesis
4: Interpret results (Consult prior knowledge) (if hypothesis rejection, jmp 2)
5: Report for peer review
6: Publish findings

Principles Driving Scientific Method
------------------------------------
Empiricism: we can understand the world and natural laws through observation
Uniformitarianism: Basic patterns exist across time and space: 
ie, erosion doesnt just stop
Parsimony: when multiple explanations are equally reasonable, the simpler is preffered 
ie Occams Razor
Uncertainty: Knowledge changes with new evidence, as do theories
Repeatability: we should be able to reproduce the same results from the same test
Proof is Elusive: we rarely expect science to provide absolute proof of a theory, 
new evidence can always change our previous knowledge
Testable questions: To find out if a theory is correct we need to test it, we cant ask a dog what it dreams

Independant/Dependant Variables
Also known as Explanatory and Response variables
Independant - Cause
Dependant - Effect

With graphs generally independant variables make up the X axis, 
the Y variable is the dependant
(functions of X change the value of Y)

Hypothesis:
Explanation for an observation
Should be proovable of falsifiable
Can be but doesnt have to be an "If...Then statement"

Observations:
What you percieve that leads to a question
More than one can pertain to the same hypothesis

A hypothesis for the decrease in NY songbirds could be
Higher noise pollution, Songbirds find mates through song, 
perhaps the increased noise from industrialization has made it more difficult to mate
Lower air quality, All/most terrestrial life requires air, 
lower quality could make it more difficult for birds to reproduce before dying

We can use models to understand environmental systems
A model is a SIMPLIFIED representation of a system
Most models are represented by equations

The goal of science is to develop theories, the more we can understand the better

Theory:
A fact about the natural world that has been proven by a large body of evidence

The scientific method may not be obvious and explicit, but it exists throughout 
daily life every moment

Examples could be:
Exploring food court options to find what is the best
Trying out different Elden Ring builds for quality and enjoyment
Using my voice around others, and tweaking it with use
